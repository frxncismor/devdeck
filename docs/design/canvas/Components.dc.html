Dialog and toast

dialog: 440–520w · radius-xl · surface-raised · shadow-lg · scrim --overlay · footer surface-2 · toast: 380w · bottom-right, 20px inset · stack gap 10 · auto-dismiss 5s (errors stay)

Dialogs trap focus, close on Esc, and put focus on Cancel first for destructive actions. The confirm button names the action and its size ("Delete 1.46 GB"), never just "OK".
